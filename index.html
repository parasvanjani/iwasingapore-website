Who We Are

A home away
from home,
in Singapore.

The Indian Women's Association (IWA) Singapore is a non-profit Voluntary Welfare Organization that brings together women of Indian origin — and women married into the Indian community — living in Singapore.

Managed entirely by a volunteer executive committee, IWA provides a warm, inclusive platform for social, cultural, educational, and philanthropic engagement.

  • Assist newcomers of Indian origin in settling into Singapore life
  • Foster friendship and cross-cultural understanding
  • Empower women through education, workshops, and enterprise
  • Nurture volunteering and community service
  • Create a sustainable network for social integration

Who We Are

A community built on
Care, Connect & Celebrate

The Indian Women's Association (IWA) Singapore is a non-profit organization functioning as a Voluntary Welfare Organization (VWO) through its elected committee and volunteer member base.

IWA comprises a large exclusive member body representative of both the expatriate as well as the local Indian community in Singapore. The Association provides a platform for women to connect, support, and network with each other whilst living in Singapore.

Through IWA, members participate in social, cultural, educational and philanthropic activities. IWA's mission is not only to present a cultural platform, but also to provide a networking interface to its working members.

Membership is open to all women of Indian origin, or married to men of Indian origin, residing in Singapore. An Associate Membership also welcomes all other women residing in Singapore.

  • 01Provide assistance and support in settling in Singapore by creating new initiatives to reach out to newcomers of Indian origin.
  • 02Encourage and foster friendship and understanding within the large growing community of Indians in Singapore.
  • 03Assist in orientation and adaptation of new citizens to Singaporean society and bring about better cross-cultural understanding.
  • 04Inculcate communication within the community and with other organizations.
  • 05Facilitate a platform for empowering women through education, workshops, events and activities that promote women's wellbeing, talents and enterprise.
  • 06Nurture and develop the spirit of volunteering and community service to make a positive and long-term impact in the community.
  • 07Create a sustainable, active network for effective social integration efforts across Singapore.

Our Journey

From a small club
to a thriving community

IWA was launched as the Expatriate Indian Women's Club on September 20, 1997 — coincidentally India's 50th year of independence. This newly formed social non-profit organization's mission was to provide a "home away from home."

It was earmarked as a support network and a link to India's rich culture, through the promotion and import of exclusively Indian events such as plays, music, art, fashion, and dance performances.

The Indian Women's Association was formally established in 1998 as a social and cultural club, providing women arriving in Singapore with a connect. IWA's activities centered around celebrating the festivals of India and bringing in artisans and luminaries of Indian culture and sports.

Originally launched as an expats group under the patronage of the High Commission of India, it was then called Expat Indian Women's Association (EIWA). The charter was subsequently changed to include all Indian women residing in Singapore, and the name was updated to Indian Women's Association Singapore to reflect this wider scope.

Membership was opened to women of Indian origin and women married to Indian men. Today, it has further opened its membership to all women who are interested in its programs and clubs through the Associate Membership.

Key Milestones
1997
Founded as Expatriate Indian Women's Club on 20 September, India's 50th Independence anniversary, under the patronage of the High Commission of India.
1998
Formally established as Indian Women's Association Singapore. First President: Julie Mehta.
Early 2000s
Expanded from expat-only to all women of Indian origin residing in Singapore. Name changed from EIWA to IWA Singapore.
2010s
Grew to 300+ members. Established I-Care partnerships with HOME, TWC2, BSS and SWAMI Home. Launched bi-annual Bazaars.
2020s
Launched Associate Membership, opening IWA to all women in Singapore regardless of origin. Established SMU Vidya Scholarship and SUTD Gyan Jyoti Study Award.
2025–27
Current committee under President Vidya Dasgupta continuing IWA's mission of Care, Connect and Celebrate across Singapore.
