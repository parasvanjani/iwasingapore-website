Who We Are

A home away
from home,
in Singapore.

The Indian Women's Association (IWA) Singapore is a non-profit Voluntary Welfare Organization that brings together women of Indian origin — and women married into the Indian community — living in Singapore.

Managed entirely by a volunteer executive committee, IWA provides a warm, inclusive platform for social, cultural, educational, and philanthropic engagement.

  • Assist newcomers of Indian origin in settling into Singapore life
  • Foster friendship and cross-cultural understanding
  • Empower women through education, workshops, and enterprise
  • Nurture volunteering and community service
  • Create a sustainable network for social integration

Who We Are

A community built on
Care, Connect & Celebrate

The Indian Women's Association (IWA) Singapore is a non-profit organization functioning as a Voluntary Welfare Organization (VWO) through its elected committee and volunteer member base.

IWA comprises a large exclusive member body representative of both the expatriate as well as the local Indian community in Singapore. The Association provides a platform for women to connect, support, and network with each other whilst living in Singapore.

Through IWA, members participate in social, cultural, educational and philanthropic activities. IWA's mission is not only to present a cultural platform, but also to provide a networking interface to its working members.

Membership is open to all women of Indian origin, or married to men of Indian origin, residing in Singapore. An Associate Membership also welcomes all other women residing in Singapore.

  • 01Provide assistance and support in settling in Singapore by creating new initiatives to reach out to newcomers of Indian origin.
  • 02Encourage and foster friendship and understanding within the large growing community of Indians in Singapore.
  • 03Assist in orientation and adaptation of new citizens to Singaporean society and bring about better cross-cultural understanding.
  • 04Inculcate communication within the community and with other organizations.
  • 05Facilitate a platform for empowering women through education, workshops, events and activities that promote women's wellbeing, talents and enterprise.
  • 06Nurture and develop the spirit of volunteering and community service to make a positive and long-term impact in the community.
  • 07Create a sustainable, active network for effective social integration efforts across Singapore.

Our Journey

From a small club
to a thriving community

IWA was launched as the Expatriate Indian Women's Club on September 20, 1997 — coincidentally India's 50th year of independence. This newly formed social non-profit organization's mission was to provide a "home away from home."

It was earmarked as a support network and a link to India's rich culture, through the promotion and import of exclusively Indian events such as plays, music, art, fashion, and dance performances.

The Indian Women's Association was formally established in 1998 as a social and cultural club, providing women arriving in Singapore with a connect. IWA's activities centered around celebrating the festivals of India and bringing in artisans and luminaries of Indian culture and sports.

Originally launched as an expats group under the patronage of the High Commission of India, it was then called Expat Indian Women's Association (EIWA). The charter was subsequently changed to include all Indian women residing in Singapore, and the name was updated to Indian Women's Association Singapore to reflect this wider scope.

Membership was opened to women of Indian origin and women married to Indian men. Today, it has further opened its membership to all women who are interested in its programs and clubs through the Associate Membership.

Key Milestones
1997
Founded as Expatriate Indian Women's Club on 20 September, India's 50th Independence anniversary, under the patronage of the High Commission of India.
1998
Formally established as Indian Women's Association Singapore. First President: Julie Mehta.
Early 2000s
Expanded from expat-only to all women of Indian origin residing in Singapore. Name changed from EIWA to IWA Singapore.
2010s
Grew to 300+ members. Established I-Care partnerships with HOME, TWC2, BSS and SWAMI Home. Launched bi-annual Bazaars.
2020s
Launched Associate Membership, opening IWA to all women in Singapore regardless of origin. Established SMU Vidya Scholarship and SUTD Gyan Jyoti Study Award.
2025–27
Current committee under President Vidya Dasgupta continuing IWA's mission of Care, Connect and Celebrate across Singapore.

Event Policies

Guest Policy — Some IWA events allow members to register the following family members as guests at member rates: member’s spouse, member’s children below 21 years old, member’s parents / parents-in-law.


Cancellation Policy — If cancellation is made within 48 hours of the event, charges are non-refundable. For cancellations prior to 48 hours: paid events — SGD $5 for members, SGD $15 for non-members. Free events — NIL for members, SGD $10 for non-members.


Non-Member Attendance — Non-members may attend a maximum of 2 IWA events per year at a minimal charge of SGD $10 per event (pricing may vary by event).


Photography Notice — Please note that photography and video recording may be made during events for publicity and administrative purposes.


Indemnity — By signing up for events you accept the IWA Indemnity Clause.

Community Service & Volunteering

💛 Acts of Kindness (AOK)

The Acts of Kindness (AOK) Club is the beating heart of IWA’s community spirit. AOK organises a diverse range of charity drives, distribution events, and volunteering initiatives that reach across Singapore’s most vulnerable communities.

From grocery drives for HOME and migrant worker outings, to festive gift pack distributions, beach clean-ups, and saree donation drives — the AOK Club gives IWA members a tangible, joyful way to give back.

The club collaborates closely with IWA’s I-Care partners including HOME, BSS, TWC2, SWAMI Home, SINDA, and other community organisations. No act of service is too small — every effort counts.

"No act of kindness, no matter how small, is ever wasted." — Aesop

☕ Adda Club

The Adda Club is IWA’s vibrant forum for stimulating conversation, debate and exchange of ideas. The word “adda” in Bengali and Hindi refers to a gathering of friends for lively, intellectually engaging chat — and that’s exactly what this club delivers.

Topics span the full spectrum of life — relationships, career, culture, technology, philosophy, society and everything in between. Sessions are warm, inclusive, and always thought-provoking.

Whether you want to challenge your perspectives, discover new viewpoints, or simply enjoy a stimulating conversation with like-minded women, Adda Club is where you belong.

"The art of conversation is the art of hearing as well as of being heard." — William Hazlitt

Literature & Reading

📚 Book Club

The IWA Book Club brings together women who share a love for the written word. Each month, members gather to discuss a carefully chosen book — spanning Indian fiction, global bestsellers, literary classics, non-fiction and more.

The club is more than just book discussions — it’s a space for cultural exchange, personal reflection, and genuine connection. Books have included works by Indian and international authors, from contemporary fiction to timeless classics.

The Book Club has also hosted author interactions and collaborated with other clubs for cross-genre events. Nominations for new books are welcome from all members.

Art, Craft & Creative Workshops

🎨 Creative Hands Club

The Creative Hands Club (CHC) is IWA’s hands-on creative workshop space. Each session introduces members to a different art form or craft technique — from Zentangle to Lippan art, Tanjore fusion, Kerala mural jewellery, silk lantern making, glass painting, and much more.

No artistic experience is required. The CHC welcomes all levels of creativity with a warm, encouraging environment. Sessions are designed to be both educational and deeply enjoyable.

The club has explored an extraordinary range of techniques over the years, celebrating both Indian craft traditions and global art forms in a uniquely IWA way.

👨‍👩‍👧 Family Club

I-Care: Giving Back

I-Care is integral to IWA’s identity — nurturing the spirit of caring, giving, and volunteerism amongst our members since day one.

BSS
Babes Pregnancy Crisis Support
BSS supports women in Singapore facing unplanned or crisis pregnancies through counselling, material assistance, and long-term care. IWA volunteers regularly fundraise and participate in BSS initiatives, including the “Sew Can We” crafting programme and cake sponsorship drives for children in their care.
Learn more →
HOME
Humanitarian Organisation for Migration Economics
HOME supports migrant workers and foreign domestic helpers in Singapore who face exploitation, abuse, or difficult circumstances. IWA members organise regular grocery drives, holiday celebrations, and volunteering sessions to support HOME’s shelter residents.
Learn more →
TWC2
Transient Workers Count Too
TWC2 advocates for the rights and welfare of low-wage migrant workers in Singapore. IWA members attend TWC2 sessions, participate in collection drives, fund movie outings for workers, and celebrate festivals like Holi and New Year with the migrant worker community.
Learn more →
SWAMI
SWAMI Home for the Elderly
The Sunshine Welfare Action Mission (SWAMI) Home provides residential care for elderly residents. IWA organises regular birthday celebrations, Christmas parties, and festive visits to bring joy and companionship to SWAMI Home residents throughout the year.
Learn more →
SMU · Annual Award
IWA Vidya Scholarship
The IWA Vidya Scholarship supports deserving students at Singapore Management University (SMU) with their educational expenses. IWA contributes annually through fundraising at Bazaars and member donations.
Learn more →
SUTD · Annual Award
Gyan Jyoti Study Award
The Gyan Jyoti Study Award supports students at the Singapore University of Technology and Design (SUTD) in technology and design disciplines — lighting the way for future innovators.
Learn more →

Membership FAQ

Women residing in Singapore over 18 years of age can join. Full Membership is for women of Indian origin or married to men of Indian origin. Associate Membership is open to all other women residing in Singapore.
Annual membership is SGD $120 per year for both Full and Associate members. Membership runs from January to December of the current year. The fee is the same regardless of which month you join.
You can pay via PayPal or credit card online, via PayNow (UEN: S97SS0085J — include your name as reference), or by cheque mailed to P.O. Box 472, Orchard Post Office, Singapore 912316. Cash payments are not accepted.
Online PayPal/credit card payments: activated within 1 week. PayNow: activated within 1 week after payment is matched. Cheque: activated after encashment, typically 4 weeks.
No. Membership fees are non-refundable and non-transferable.
Existing members do not need to fill the form again. Simply log in, click Renewal, verify your details, and proceed to pay. New members must complete the full application form.
Associate Membership is open to women residing in Singapore who are neither of Indian origin nor married to men of Indian origin. Associate Members enjoy all member privileges except the right to vote or hold office in IWA.
In the interest of going green, IWA does not print membership cards. You will have an e-card available on your membership login as your identifier. Show a screenshot at Privilege Partner outlets.
Non-members may attend a maximum of 2 IWA events per year at SGD $10 per event (price may vary). Members-only events require an activated membership.
