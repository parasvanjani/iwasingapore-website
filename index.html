Who We Are

A home away
from home,
in Singapore.

The Indian Women's Association (IWA) Singapore is a non-profit Voluntary Welfare Organization that brings together women of Indian origin — and women married into the Indian community — living in Singapore.

Managed entirely by a volunteer executive committee, IWA provides a warm, inclusive platform for social, cultural, educational, and philanthropic engagement.

  • Assist newcomers of Indian origin in settling into Singapore life
  • Foster friendship and cross-cultural understanding
  • Empower women through education, workshops, and enterprise
  • Nurture volunteering and community service
  • Create a sustainable network for social integration

Community Service

I-Care: Giving back

IWA's I-Care initiative partners with leading welfare organisations to make a tangible difference in the lives of those who need it most.

01
BSS
Babes Pregnancy Crisis Support
Supporting women facing pregnancy crises through counselling, material aid, and compassionate care.
02
HOME
Humanitarian Organisation for Migration Economics
Grocery drives and welfare support for migrant workers and foreign domestic helpers in Singapore.
03
TWC2
Transient Workers Count Too
Championing fair treatment and rights of low-wage migrant workers through advocacy and direct support.
04
SWAMI
SWAMI Home for the Elderly
Bringing joy to elderly residents through regular visits, celebrations, and personal connection.
05
SMU
IWA Vidya Scholarship
Annual scholarship supporting deserving students at Singapore Management University with their education.
06
SUTD
Gyan Jyoti Study Award
Illuminating futures — the IWA SUTD award empowers driven students in technology and design.
